# WET-EXEC-010 / 011 — GAMMA IMAGE PROMPTS
## Canonical source for diagram composition · 42 cards

**This file is never pasted into Gamma as a whole.** Work card by card: paste the **diagram prompt** into Gamma's image or diagram field for that card, then apply the **layout prompt** if the card needs repositioning.

**Geometry is meaning.** Where a prompt fixes a count, an order, an axis, a proportion or an emptiness, that constraint is the argument. Gamma may restyle freely; it may not restructure. A prompt that comes back with a different number of tiers, a resolved ambiguity, or a filled void has produced a different claim.

**Global prefix — prepend to every prompt below:**

> Minimal executive keynote diagram. Near-black ground, deep navy panels, slate connectors, one blue accent for evidenced items and one gold accent for open items. Thin single-weight lines, generous spacing, wide margins, no shadows, no gradients, no glow. No people, no photography, no robots, no brains, no circuit boards, no servers, no handshakes, no icons unless specified. Structure and typography only.

**Diagram prompt.** Five links in a single vertical descent, centred, with generous vertical spacing. Links one through four connected by solid connectors and rendered in the blue accent. **The fifth link is rendered differently — outline rather than fill, reached by a dashed connector** — because it is the only line in the deck written in the future tense. No other element on the card.

**Infographic prompt.** *The Documentary proved the platform.* → *The Platform produced knowledge.* → *The Knowledge became the asset.* → *The Governance became the differentiator.* ⇢ *The Future will determine the legacy.*

**Layout prompt.** **No ascending arrow, no upward curve, no growth motif, no rising staircase.** A descent that ends in something not yet earned is the correct shape. Nothing follows this card.
