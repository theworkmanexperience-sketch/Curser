## 11 · WHAT THE TIMELINE SHOWS THAT NO SINGLE DOCUMENT DOES

**Governance and engineering intensified together.** The densest engineering era (III, 68 commits) and the densest governance era (VI, 56 commits in three days) are both peaks, not trade-offs. **22 August is simultaneously the densest day in the repository and a pure governance day.**

**The silence is load-bearing.** Eleven days with no commits sit exactly between the platform's engineering maturity (Era IV closes at 404 tests) and its constitutional turn (Era V opens with the corpus). The gap is where Part 1 shipped without the platform, and every instrument in Eras V–VII traces back to it.

**Findings cluster immediately before ratifications.** F1/F2 on 12 August; the 20 clauses on 13–15 August. The `191/191` discovery on 29 August; `ER-006` and `ER-007` the same week. **The corpus grows when the platform is wrong, not when it is working.**
