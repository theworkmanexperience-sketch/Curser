## 6.1 · Why the evidence now points where it did not before `[E]`

**`CF-001_EVIDENCE_REVIEW` scored five criteria five-to-nil toward GT-2 `89d61f96`.** The load-bearing criterion was **lineage** — GT-2 is the caption export of `2bf06853`, which was then the governed editorial timeline.

**`ED-003` changed that fact.** The governed editorial timeline for the 08-24 lineage is now `d82c2c3e`, and **the caption stream that pairs with it by directory and filename stem is `d93d86a1`** — the same pairing method by which GT-2's lineage was established. **`2bf06853` is `SUPERSEDED_ASSEMBLY` and cannot carry the 08-24 lineage.**

**The determination at §2.1 is therefore consistent with the evidence as it now stands, and inconsistent with the evidence as it stood before `ED-003`.** **Both states are recorded so the change is visible as a consequence of a designation rather than as a reversal.**

## 6.2 · The citation population now indexes a third stream `[E]`

```
declared before CF-001    GT-2       89d61f96    2,291 cues
actually indexed          c13df1f4   c13df1f4    2,290 cues
designated at §2.1        d93d86a1   d93d86a1    2,034 cues
```

**The 91 governed citations index `c13df1f4`, which is neither the previously declared stream nor the stream designated here.** **§3 provides that this determination does not repoint them, and it does not.**

**Recorded so the state is visible rather than discovered later:** after issuance the citations will index a stream that is not canonical, against a canonical stream with 256 fewer cues. **Resolving that is `ED-004`'s and `ECR-003`'s territory, and no part of it is decided here.**

## 6.3 · The designated lineage contains a more complete caption record `[E]`

**The designated Picture Lock `d82c2c3e` carries 3,003 caption elements natively, across five lanes. The designated stream carries 2,034 cues — 73.9 % of those events; 784 are not represented.**

**Both artifacts are inside the designated 08-24 lineage.** §2.3 acknowledges the lossiness and expressly does not let it diminish the authority created here.

**Recorded at issuance, not as an objection:** **designating the SRT makes the Canonical Caption Stream a lossy view of a more complete record held inside the already-designated Picture Lock.** **The Executive has made this designation with that property on the record: the SRT governs as the machine-readable caption stream, while the designated Picture Lock retains the complete caption source within the same lineage.**
