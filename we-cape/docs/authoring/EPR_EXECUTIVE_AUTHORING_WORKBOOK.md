## 1 · The vocabulary

`dramatic_intensity` accepts **exactly these five tokens**, ratified and reaffirmed by the Order
of 2026-08-28. No sixth value is valid. No blend, range, or arrow is valid.

```
LOW          MODERATE          HIGH          ELEVATED          CLIMACTIC
```

The descriptors below are **transcribed from EPR-001 v1.2.0**, where they were already ratified.
They are the registry's own words, not the platform's gloss:

| token | ratified descriptors |
|---|---|
| `LOW` | Reflective · Observational |
| `MODERATE` | Engaged · Conversational |
| `HIGH` | Purposeful · Energetic |
| `ELEVATED` | Commanding · Emotionally Significant |
| `CLIMACTIC` | Defining · Irreversible |

> ### ⚠ The order they are printed in is not a ranking
>
> **`intensity_scale.ordered` is still `AWAITING_EXECUTIVE_INPUT`.** The tokens are listed above
> in the sequence your Order gives them, and that sequence carries **no** claim that `ELEVATED`
> is above `HIGH`, or that `LOW` is the least of anything. **Section 5 asks you to settle it.**
>
> This matters while you answer: if the scale turns out to be **labelled**, each beat's token is
> a name for a quality. If it turns out to be **ordered**, the tokens you choose across six beats
> also describe a shape. **You may wish to answer Section 5 first.**

## Appendix B · What happens to your answers

1. Returned values are **transcribed verbatim** into EPR-001. Not normalised, not tidied, not
   reworded. `EPR-05`'s arrow survived two transcriptions this way.
2. `epr_validate.py` re-runs V-1…V-6 — **structure only.** `V-6` checks that any
   `dramatic_intensity` is one of the five ratified tokens. **That is a vocabulary check. It
   cannot tell whether the token you chose is right, and nothing in this platform can.**
3. `EPR-001`'s `registry_version` increments and the file returns to you for **formal
   ratification.**
4. **Only after that ratification** does Order §4 release the `gen_artifacts.py` RUN_ID lock.
   Until then: no regeneration, no registry population, no Conductor Score.

**Blank fields stay blank.** `missing_data_policy: propagate_unknown` — an unanswered question
propagates as unknown and is never defaulted, in this round or any later one.
