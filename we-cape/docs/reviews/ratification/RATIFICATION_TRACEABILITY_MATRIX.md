# The one exception, named

**`A-8b` is the only amendment whose wording did not originate with the Executive.**

`ECO-001` Clarification 4 accepts all eight updates in principle and expressly authorizes *"new definitions."* **That grants authority for the amendment. It does not ratify the specific text**, which was drafted by the engineering channel and flagged as such when first delivered:

> `CAM-001` §3 `S-8`: *"`Artifact kind` is proposed, not transcribed… The definition above is the one consistent with Model A; **it is flagged because it is mine, not yours.**"*

**Why it cannot be waved through.** The definition settles that `CONDUCTOR_SCORE` and `CONDUCTOR_SCORE v1.1` are the **same kind** and therefore compete for one canonical slot. **The alternative reading — that a version change creates a new kind — would let every version be canonical in its own right and make `U-1` do no work.**

**Status is therefore `REQUIRES EXECUTIVE ADOPTION` rather than `READY`.** Ratification of this amendment is a positive act, not a tick.
