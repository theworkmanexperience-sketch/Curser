## Trigger
The Chairman declares PICTURE LOCK: no further cuts, trims, or
re-orders. Sound, music, VO, and graphics may still change; timing
may not.

## Phase A — Lock (immediately at declaration)
A1 Fresh FCPXML export of the locked timeline → XML/
   (File > Export XML; name P2_LOCK_<date>.fcpxml). One export,
   four instruments: ER-1 closure · utilization input · IAR
   runtime evidence · Panel-7 timeline column. It is also MIE's
   pacing evidence — the timing contract for all cue work.
A2 Edit clock STOP: part2_edit_clock closed in BOTH shoot.yaml
   manifests with total hours; Criterion-2 tally updated
   (curation 38 · P1 edit 12 · P2 edit final · vs RWP 64).
A3 Utilization report: locked timeline vs the 80-clip pool and
   X5 lineage record (the Part-1 1.9% instrument, rerun).
A4 GATE 1 TIMELINE AUDIT (recorded): parse the lock XML — every
   clip resolves to ORIGINAL or cleared-CONTRIBUTED custody
   (currently cleared: NOTOR1OUS ×2 only). Any uncleared clip =
   remove or obtain consent before proceeding. No exceptions.

## Phase B — Score & Sound (the MIE window)
B1 Timeline timing is FROZEN; MIE/soundtrack/VO work proceeds
   against the lock XML. Cue selections land as PDRs per
   SEM-001/EA-001 (custody-hashed lyrics/style prompts,
   generation records, rights ledger).
B2 Any picture change during this window voids the lock:
   return to A1 (re-export, re-audit). Deltas explained, always.

## Phase C — Publication Gates (recorded BEFORE upload)
C1 Final render → Final Video/ (named, dated).
C2 GATE 2 — claims/content review recorded (proclamation names,
   civic claims, on-screen text vs evidence; UNCONFIRMED registry
   items may not appear as asserted fact).
C3 GATE 3 — Chairman publication approval recorded; retention
   clocks armed (distribution + 12 months); AI-disclosure
   verified (Suno cues, YouTube synthetic-media declaration).
C4 Publish. Publication record filed in both manifests
   (platform, URL, runtime, render hash).

## Reconciliation
Every phase boundary reports counts (clips in lock vs pool;
cues proposed vs approved vs placed) — no unexplained deltas.
