## I. Purpose

This document defines the architectural vision for the Editorial
Intelligence Stack: a layered system of intelligence capabilities that
transforms governed production evidence into editorial understanding,
narrative insight, musical intelligence, and publication products.

The stack formalizes what the Alpha RoundUp productions have proven by
hand: that a documentary production generates structured, extractable,
governable knowledge — and that this knowledge compounds across
productions when captured in registries rather than left in raw media.

## II. The Four Engines

The stack is composed of four intelligence engines, each answering one
question:

**DIE — Documentary Intelligence Engine: "What exists?"**
The foundation layer. DIE extracts and registers ground-truth facts
from canonical production sources (transcripts, video, telemetry):
riders, motorcycles, interviews, questions, answers, organizations,
locations, quotes, timeline segments. DIE does not interpret. It
records. Its outputs are the registries — the governed factual
substrate every higher engine consumes.

**NIE — Narrative Intelligence Engine: "Why does it matter?"**
The meaning layer. NIE consumes DIE registries and identifies themes,
arcs, motifs, emotional threads, and story structure — the "wind
therapy" motif, the brotherhood cluster, the legacy thread. NIE does
not generate creative content. It generates editorial recommendations:
narrative arc proposals, strategic silence recommendations, VO
placement recommendations, editorial caption recommendations.

**MIE — Musical Intelligence Engine: "How should it feel?"**
The emotional layer. MIE consumes DIE facts and NIE narrative structure
to produce musical intelligence: cue placement recommendations, tempo
and energy mapping, thematic scoring guidance, lyric candidates, and
Suno style packages aligned to narrative beats.

**PIE — Publication Intelligence Engine: "What products?"**
The distribution layer. PIE consumes all lower tiers to propose
publication products: episode structures, shorts and clip candidates,
social content, chaptering, titles, descriptions, and audience-facing
derivatives.

## III. The Stack

Ground Truth (canonical sources) is consumed by DIE (What exists?),
whose governed outputs feed NIE (Why does it matter?), whose outputs
feed MIE (How should it feel?), whose outputs feed PIE (What
products?), which proposes Publication Products.

Each engine consumes the governed outputs of the layers beneath it.
Intelligence is progressive: facts before meaning, meaning before
feeling, feeling before products.

## IV. Evidence Discipline

The stack inherits W.E.I.C.P.'s evidence-first philosophy:

- Every extracted fact carries source citation (timecode, document,
  registry row).
- Observed facts, derived conclusions, recommendations, and generated
  content are distinct evidence classes and are labeled as such.
- Uncertain attributions are marked UNCONFIRMED, never silently
  resolved.
- No engine invents. Every output traces to governed evidence.

## V. The Registry Model

DIE's registries are the stack's foundation and the platform's
long-term knowledge asset:

Rider/Interviewee Registry, Motorcycle Registry, Interview Registry,
Question Registry, "Why I Ride" Registry, Organization Registry,
Location Registry, Quote Library, Timeline Intelligence Index.

Registries are per-production at extraction and cross-production at
accumulation: the same rider appearing at multiple roundups, the same
question asked across years, the evolution of answers over time. This
enables longitudinal editorial intelligence no single production
could produce.

## VI. Proven Foundation

This vision is not speculative. The Part 2 Canonical SRT Registry
Extraction (2026-08-20) exercised the DIE layer end-to-end on a real
production: 75 rider interviews, 9 registries, timecode-cited,
evidence-classed, UNCONFIRMED-disciplined. The extraction demonstrated
both the capability and the necessity of the governance this stack
formalizes.

## VII. Relationship to Existing Governance

The stack operates within W.E.I.C.P.'s established constitutional
framework: PDRs capture decisions; gates govern rights and
publication; specifications define schemas and validation; the
registry provides custody.

The engines analyze, recommend, and generate governed artifacts within
their authorized scope. Production decisions remain the responsibility
of human governance through the Production Decision Record (PDR)
process and established publication gates.

## VIII. Principle of Human Editorial Authority

No Editorial Intelligence engine possesses production authority. All
recommendations, analyses, and generated artifacts remain advisory
until accepted through governed human decision-making and recorded in
a Production Decision Record (PDR).

This principle complements Evidence First, Progressive Intelligence,
and Transcript Authority, and makes the constitutional boundary
unmistakable.

## IX. What This Document Is Not

This is an architectural vision, not a specification. It defines
