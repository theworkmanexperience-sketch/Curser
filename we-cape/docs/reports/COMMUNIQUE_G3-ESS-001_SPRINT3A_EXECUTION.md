# Executive Statement
The Executive Team has completed its review of G3-ESS-001 Rev A,
ADR-009, and the associated governance updates. Sprint 3A is hereby
authorized. This communiqué defines the Executive Team's expectations,
constraints, deliverables, and review criteria prior to implementation.

# Executive Objective
Sprint 3A is **not** a music-generation sprint. Its purpose is to prove
that the WE CAPE Platform can automatically transform governed
documentary intelligence into a governed Editorial Synchronization
layer and an executable Conductor's Score suitable for professional
soundtrack production. Success will be measured by traceability,
documentary fidelity, reproducibility, and governance—not by music
generation.

# Executive Expectations
## 1. Editorial Accuracy
Execute Step 0 first. Resolve the ±6-second timing tolerance before
making any frame-accuracy claim. Frame accuracy is earned through
reconciliation.
## 2. Documentary Fidelity
Visual analysis operates as DIE-V under ADR-009. Visual observations
reinforce governed documentary evidence. Visual observations shall
never supersede governed registries. Where confidence is insufficient,
classify observations rather than infer conclusions.
## 3. Editorial Synchronization
Produce a governed EDITORIAL_SYNCHRONIZATION.yaml that fuses: Editorial
Timing Contract · Documentary Progression · Interview Registry ·
Voice-over Registry · Caption Registry · Energy Curve · Visual Event
Registry (DIE-V). The artifact shall accurately describe how the
documentary unfolds.

## 4. Conductor's Score
Produce an executable CONDUCTOR_SCORE.yaml. It shall define musical
behavior—not musical content—including: Cue boundaries · Transition
behaviors · Ducking · Rebuild timing · Instrumentation guidance ·
Road Soul(TM) behavioral assignments · Lyric opportunity windows.
No music generation is authorized.
## 5. Validation
Produce sufficient evidence demonstrating synchronization integrity,
reproducibility, and constitutional compliance. No unexplained deltas.

# Executive Constraints
Operate under ADR-009 · Preserve documentary authority · No biometric
identification · No unsupported sentiment inference · No undocumented
assumptions · No unexplained deltas.

# Executive Review Criteria
1. Technical Integrity · 2. Editorial Integrity · 3. Musical Readiness
· 4. Production Readiness · 5. Constitutional Compliance ·

# Success Criteria
Sprint 3A is successful when: editorial authority is preserved ·
synchronization is machine-readable · visual reinforcement
strengthens—not replaces—documentary intelligence · the Conductor's
Score is executable · every artifact is evidence-linked and
reproducible.

# Closing
The objective of Sprint 3A is to demonstrate that WE CAPE can observe,
synchronize, and prepare a locked documentary for soundtrack
orchestration while preserving governance, documentary fidelity, and
executive trust. This sprint establishes the operational bridge
between Documentary Intelligence and Musical Intelligence. The
Executive Team appreciates your engineering partnership and looks
forward to reviewing the completed artifacts.
