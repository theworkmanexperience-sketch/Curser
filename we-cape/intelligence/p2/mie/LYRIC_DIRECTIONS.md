# Lyric Directions — Four Voices for the Documentary to Choose Among
*Per Executive Review: options before selection. All original writing on registered themes; no identifiable person quoted; "wind therapy" used as the film's communal motif (17 independent speakers). GENERATED-class candidates for EP curation; selection lands as a cue PDR with consent check.*

## Direction 1 — Documentary Anthem (narrative, mid-tempo, plainspoken)
**CUE-10 hook:** *Two wheels down and the world gets quiet / call it wind therapy — brother, don't deny it / from the ones before us to the ones we raise / we ride it forward, homecoming days.*
**Escort treatment:** riff-forward, single declaimed line: *Wheels up — the whole town knows our name today.*
**Character:** the narrator's voice; states the thesis directly; safest, most universal.

## Direction 2 — Southern Trap (808 warmth, chant hook, night-forward)
**CUE-10 hook:** *Seventy deep when we slide through / lights on the chrome, that's the vibe too / therapy in the throttle, family in the smoke / we been riding on a promise that'll never be broke.*
**Escort treatment:** sparse 808 pulse under chant: *Roll call — every brother present.*
**Character:** youngest energy in the room; strongest for CELEBRATION family crossover; boldest brand statement.

## Direction 3 — Gospel / Soul (choir lift, testimony structure, resolve)
**CUE-10 hook:** *I found my peace where the road runs wide / (he found his peace) with my brothers at my side / every mile a mercy, every ride a praise / carry it home now — homecoming days.*
**Escort treatment:** call-and-response horn line, wordless choir swell only.
**Character:** matches the film's reverence spine (proclamations, honors, the sacred minute); the emotional ceiling.

## Direction 4 — Blues / Americana (slide guitar, worn voice, storyteller)
**CUE-10 hook:** *Thirty-seven years and I still can't say / what not riding means — guess it means I stay / wind for my worries, brothers for my load / everything I needed, I found on this road.*
**Escort treatment:** instrumental slide motif only; the road talks.
**Character:** the elder's voice (R34's 37 years, R43's "since I was 13" — themes, not quotes); most timeless; pairs with REFLECTION family.
